# v9.3.5 — Cinematic Orchestral Score

Replaces the v9.3.4 sustained ambient drone with an original, procedural space-opera score.

- Original harmony/melody only; no franchise themes or copied melodies.
- Layered synthesized strings, low brass, bass, melodic lead, pizzicato/ostinato, timpani and cymbal/noise percussion.
- Scene-aware arrangements for Overview, Story, Holonet, Ship, Combat and Dogfight.
- Story/Overview are slower and restrained; Combat/Dogfight raise tempo, ostinato density and percussion.
- Music still ducks automatically beneath NPC spoken dialogue.
- Generated entirely with Web Audio: no streaming, no audio asset download, no API, no subscription.
