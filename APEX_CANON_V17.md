# The 3.2 Apex Protocol — recovered campaign canon

Rovan Kes, callsign Deadlock, is founder and leader of **The 3.2 Apex Protocol**, his crime syndicate and bounty-hunter clan. **3.2 commemorates the successful 3.2-million-credit heist**, explicitly clarified by the player during this update.

## Established ownership and founding priorities

Blackglass Station and two Kestrel F-4 interceptors were acquired together for 650,000 credits. Establishment work was budgeted at 780,000 credits. The seller signed an NDA. Blackglass is a decommissioned repair installation buried inside an asteroid; only its armored docking throat breaks the rock’s surface.

Priorities were a secure headquarters, Jessa’s research, weapons armory, hangar and fighters; cargo ships later. The recovered reserve after establishment is 1,420,000 credits, separate from the personal snapshot of 358,400 credits. These are historical balances, not additive payouts. The heist is not awarded a second time.

## Blackglass assets

- Completed laboratory, armory, weapons workshop and test chamber.
- Isolated data vault; Blackwake’s blueprint archived. The real Varo wafer remains in the personal ship’s concealed compartment, not silently moved to the vault.
- Main hangar with two repair positions.
- Rebuilt access defenses, turrets, sensors and lockdowns.
- Flight-ready Kestrel F-4 interceptors **128 and 129**; 129 repaired. **Permanent pilots unappointed.** No invented fighter combat statistics or deployment orders.
- Expansion cavern could become a cargo berth; cargo fleet and completed expansion remain unestablished.

## Unresolved decisions and source boundaries

A formal charter, additional members, and permanent fighter pilots remain open. The original record did not supply exact galaxy/system coordinates. The game’s existing Blackglass node in Kaelor is retained as map placement, not asserted as recovered tabletop geography.

The later game’s Raven name and current ship mechanics are retained. The visual tabletop sheet’s unnamed Rook-class cutter and homebrew combat values are not used to silently replace current game progression or rebalance combat during this syndicate update.

The recovered Rusk briefing is archival: 160,000 alive / 80,000 dead; +40,000 for an intact convoy; six in-game hours remained at that snapshot. Acceptance, partner fees and deployment were undecided. This release does not start that clock, accept the contract or pay the contacts.

## What v17 implements

Persistent founder identity, Blackglass ownership, facility and personnel records, separate treasury with validated transfers, transaction history, context-sensitive Jessa briefings, and optional Apex identity for **new Lower Port cases**. Signed cases route rewards to the reserve; residents learn disclosed affiliation and remember it. Public outcomes affect a new local standing measure. This is a game mechanic starting at zero, not a fabricated historic reputation score.

Old saves gain the 1,420,000-credit reserve once and retain their current personal balance. Fresh campaigns start with the recovered 358,400 personal snapshot. Existing local jobs stay personal; changing future case identity never rewrites an accepted case. Historical heist/purchase costs are never replayed. Reloading preserves case generation and treasury state.

This is a foundation for syndicate play, not a full fleet, hiring, territory or autonomous empire simulator. No contacts are conscripted or pilot roles invented. The procedural job pool remains finite and expandable.
