# v14 scene artwork

Five original environment plates generated with the built-in image generation tool for this release. Existing character portraits and legacy SVG assets were preserved.

| File in assets/scenes | Art direction |
|---|---|
| blackglass_command_v14.png | Cinematic orbital command lounge overlooking a planet, warm interior and cool space lighting. |
| raven_cockpit_v14.png | Detailed spacecraft cockpit, forward space view, cinematic science-fiction materials and lighting. |
| veyra_port_v14.png | Rainy neon futuristic lower spaceport, industrial city architecture. |
| veyra_upper_v14.png | Sunlit megacity terrace, pale-metal towers, skybridges, hanging gardens, turquoise glass and cream stone. |
| veyra_undercity_v14.png | Industrial street beneath a city, rusty pipes, cables, damp steel stairs, red maintenance lights and cyan vents. |

All plates were requested as cohesive landscape environments without text, interface, logos, borders or collage. This table summarizes the generation direction, rather than claiming to preserve every prompt verbatim.
