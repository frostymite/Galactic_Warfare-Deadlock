# Raven Rising artwork

Created with the built-in image generation tool. Original v14 art is retained.

## Raven sprite

Saved project asset: `assets/ships/raven_cutout_v15.png`

Prompt:

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent game ship sprite for the Deadlock RPG, displayed over animated space. Subject: THE RAVEN, a rugged compact bounty-hunter light freighter / fighter hybrid. Broad low wedge hull, angular obsidian armored plating, asymmetric utilitarian modules, electric-cyan cockpit slit and small cyan running lights, two rear engine housings, weathered gunmetal panels, restrained copper repairs, visible dorsal cannon and cargo section. Three-quarter elevated side view, nose pointing RIGHT, engines on LEFT, complete ship fully within frame with generous transparent margin, no extreme foreshortening. Detailed cinematic realistic sci-fi game asset, strong readable silhouette, cool soft studio rim lighting and clean sharp texture. Ship only on genuinely transparent background; no stars, no planet, no ground or drop shadow, no lettering, labels, border or watermark. Engine apertures softly lit cyan, no long exhaust trails (animated in game). Wide landscape composition.

## Orbital backdrop

Saved project asset: `assets/scenes/veyra_orbit_v15.png`

Prompt:

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: 16:9 cinematic environment background for the Deadlock RPG flight deck; ships will be composited by the game. Scene: high orbit above Veyra Prime, a luminous blue-white planetary crescent curves across the lower right of a vast dark starfield. Fine gold city lights on the night side, wispy atmosphere, distant small moons, subtle indigo nebulae, a few tiny faraway orbital station silhouettes along the horizon. Center and left remain mostly dark open space to make a foreground spaceship readable. Rich realistic science-fiction matte painting, dramatic but restrained, electric cyan atmosphere and cool midnight navy with warm gold city lights. No foreground ships, no text, no interface, no grid, no logos, no borders or collage. Wide landscape.

## Editable vectors

The following SVG files were authored in code for crisp tactical displays. They are schematic illustrations, not measured engineering plans or 3D models.

- `assets/ships/raven_vector_v15.svg`: Raven dorsal schematic.
- `assets/ships/courier_vector_v15.svg`: Needle courier.
- `assets/ships/patrol_vector_v15.svg`: Warden patrol cutter.
- `assets/ships/beacon_vector_v15.svg`: navigation relay.

The transparent Raven is composited in the browser over the orbital plate or a CSS deep-space backdrop. CSS transforms animate the ship, stars and engine glow. Existing character portraits remain unchanged.
