# Deadlock v10.1 — Forced Encounters

## Purpose
Danger now has mechanical consequences. Story and travel cannot always be clicked through safely.

## Ground combat triggers
- hostile story complications at high danger
- failed final target recovery approaches
- explicit player escalation to live fire
- probabilistic ambushes at high-danger local sites

## Space combat triggers
- hostile travel events (rival hunters / pirate probes) that are ignored or fail resolution

## Forced encounter behavior
- Story/Galaxy navigation is locked to Combat or Dogfight until resolved
- initiative is rolled automatically for generated ground encounters
- hostile ground combatants take automatic turns and damage Rovan
- player can fire, take cover, or attempt to break contact
- enemy ship volleys damage Raven shields/hull after player dogfight actions
- player can destroy hostiles or break pursuit

## Danger bands
- 0–39: Low Threat
- 40–64: Active Threat
- 65–84: High Threat; hostile complications may force combat
- 85–100: Combat Imminent; the next hostile beat can force an encounter

## Campaign safety
Defeat does not automatically kill Rovan or destroy The Raven. It produces an emergency retreat, injury/ship damage, and higher danger so the campaign continues.
