# Deadlock v9.3.4 — Ambient Soundscape & Character Voices

- Adds a subtle procedural Web Audio ambient bed; no music files or network connection required.
- Ambient tone adapts slightly to Story, Holonet, Combat, Dogfight, Ship, and current danger.
- Ambient audio ducks automatically while NPC dialogue is spoken.
- Adds device text-to-speech for Dren, Nera Kade, Renn, Jessa, and unidentified Holonet senders.
- Rovan remains unvoiced because the player controls him.
- Each major NPC uses a distinct pitch/rate/voice selection profile.
- Adds master sound, ambient music, music volume, NPC voice, and voice-volume controls.
- Android offline NPC speech depends on an installed/downloaded system TTS voice.
- Music generation itself is fully local and offline.
