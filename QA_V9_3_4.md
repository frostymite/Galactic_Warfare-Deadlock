## Device note
Exact speech timbre is supplied by the Android/browser text-to-speech engine and cannot be acoustically validated in headless Chromium. The game prefers local device voices and applies distinct pitch/rate profiles per NPC. Offline spoken dialogue requires an Android TTS voice available offline. Procedural background music does not require any downloaded audio asset or network connection.
