# Lower Port environment artwork

Four original 16:9 environment plates were created with the built-in image generation tool. The existing landing-bay plate and all previous artwork remain available. Animated rain, steam, light and a passing courier are separate browser layers; these are not 3D environments.

## market

Project asset: `assets/scenes/port_market_v16.svg`

Exact prompt:

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: 16:9 landscape environment plate for Deadlock, a cinematic sci-fi RPG. Lower Port freight market on Veyra Prime, rainy futuristic bazaar wedged beneath colossal industrial cargo gantries. Covered food and parts stalls, warm amber lanterns, cyan shop lighting, layered catwalks, wet metal paving, tiny distant pedestrians, an open central walking path. Eye-level player perspective, no large foreground characters. Detailed realistic science-fiction matte painting, weathered materials, atmospheric depth, strong readable composition with room for UI outside the artwork. Obsidian, electric cyan and warm amber palette. One cohesive scene. No text, readable signs, UI, borders, watermark, collage or foreground weapon.
