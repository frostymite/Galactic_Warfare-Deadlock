# v9.4 Guided Hunt Loop

## Why
The old contract progress percentage was mechanically useful but did not tell the player what they were accomplishing or what to do next.

## Mission phases
For target hunts: Lead Gathering (0–24), Build the Trail (25–49), Trail Narrowed (50–74), Search Window (75–89), Target Located (90–99), Target Secured / Turn-In (100).

The percentage remains a rules value called Trail Confidence. Crossing a threshold changes the available gameplay.

## Choice clarity
Each choice now declares its purpose (Gain Intel, Work Contact, Press Lead, Bait Network, Secure Target, etc.) and explains the expected type of consequence.

## Resolution
Target-recovery contracts cap ordinary investigation at 95%. Once the target is located, a successful capture approach sets the objective to 100% and unlocks turn-in. Turn-in pays the gross contract reward.
