# Blackglass Station — Deadlock HQ

Blackglass Station is the established headquarters for Deadlock.

The v10 gameplay default includes:

- **Command Lounge** — tactical display, encrypted comms, campaign intelligence.
- **Briefing Room** — target dossiers and mission planning.
- **Rovan's Quarters** — journal, identities, gear, secure personal storage.
- **Hangar Access** — The Raven, diagnostics, repairs, weapons-bench access.
- **Contract Board** — filtered bounty and contract intake.

The station remains accessible through a secure remote link while The Raven is away. Physical-access status changes automatically with navigation position.

Only the existence of Blackglass Station as Deadlock HQ is treated as newly established canon from this build request. Interior details remain editable worldbuilding defaults until further established in play.
